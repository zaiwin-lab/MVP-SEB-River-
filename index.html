Let's Bring Our Rivers Back to Life! 🌱 Jom Hidupkan Semula Sungai Kita! 🌱 让我们一起复活河流！🌱 Mai Kitai Ngemulihka Sungai Kitai! 🌱

Sungai Revive is a fun, hands-on river rehabilitation programme for Sarawak — where students, teachers and communities learn, play, and take real action to heal our rivers together.

Sungai Revive ialah program pemulihan sungai yang seronok dan praktikal untuk Sarawak — di mana pelajar, guru dan komuniti belajar, bermain, dan mengambil tindakan sebenar untuk memulihkan sungai kita bersama.

Sungai Revive 是砂拉越一项有趣且注重实践的河流修复计划——学生、教师和社区在这里一起学习、玩乐，并采取实际行动共同修复我们的河流。

Sungai Revive ke program pemulih sungai ke pengelantang ke Sarawak — pelajar, guru enggau komuniti belajar, main, sereta ngaul tindak ulih nyaup sungai kitai sama-sama.

Research & Innovation

Science-backed river rehabilitation 🔬

Sungai Revive uses proven bioremediation technologies. Here's the science behind the magic.

⚫

Biochar Technology

A carbon-rich material made through pyrolysis. Added to mudballs, it boosts microbial activity, absorbs pollutants, and increases dissolved oxygen in rivers.

↑DO
Dissolved Oxygen
↓BOD
Oxygen Demand
🦠

Effective Microorganisms (EM)

A blend of beneficial microbes — photosynthetic bacteria, lactic acid bacteria, and yeast — that break down pollutants and restore river ecology.

↓NH₃
Ammonia
↓COD
Chemical Demand
🧱

Mudball Technology

Biochar + EM + clay soil + organic matter, hand-formed into balls that slowly release good microbes into the riverbed.

Water Quality
6–8 wks
Release Duration

Water Quality, Simply Explained 💧

We track these 5 parameters before and after every mudball deployment.

DO
💧 Dissolved Oxygen

Oxygen for fish and aquatic life. Higher = healthier. Target: >7 mg/L

BOD
🦠 Biochemical Oxygen Demand

Oxygen needed to break down organic matter. Lower = cleaner.

COD
⚗️ Chemical Oxygen Demand

Measures chemical pollutants. Lower = better water quality.

NH₃
🧪 Ammonia Nitrogen

Toxic to aquatic life at high levels — a sign of sewage or waste runoff.

WQI
📊 Water Quality Index

Combined score: Class I (>92.7) to Class V (<30.9). We target Class II+.
