Why It Matters

Rivers are Sarawak's lifeline. Sungai adalah urat nadi Sarawak. 河流是 砂拉越 的生命线。 Sungai ke nyawa Sarawak.

Our rivers are under threat from pollution, deforestation, and unsustainable practices. Sungai Revive addresses this through five integrated pillars. Sungai kita terancam oleh pencemaran, penebangan hutan, dan amalan tidak mampan. Sungai Revive menangani ini melalui lima tiang bersepadu. 我们的河流正面临污染、森林砍伐和不可持续做法的威胁。Sungai Revive 通过五个综合支柱应对这一挑战。 Sungai kitai diancam pencemaran, penebangan kayu enggau cara nadai mampan. Sungai Revive ngelaban nya ari lima bida.

Research & Innovation

Science-backed river rehabilitation.

Sungai Revive uses proven bioremediation technologies. Here's the science behind the movement.

⚫

Biochar Technology

Biochar is a carbon-rich material produced from organic matter through pyrolysis. When added to mudballs, it improves microbial activity, absorbs pollutants, and increases dissolved oxygen levels in rivers.

↑DO
Dissolved Oxygen
↓BOD
Biochemical Oxygen Demand
🦠

Effective Microorganisms (EM)

A blend of beneficial microorganisms including photosynthetic bacteria, lactic acid bacteria, and yeast. EM breaks down organic pollutants, reduces ammonia, and restores natural river ecology.

↓NH₃
Ammonia
↓COD
Chemical Oxygen Demand
🧱

Mudball Technology

Combining biochar, EM, clay soil, and organic matter into hand-formed balls that slowly release beneficial microorganisms into the river bed, targeting polluted sediments at the source.

↑WQI
Water Quality Index
6–8 wks
Release Duration

Water Quality Indicators — Simply Explained

We track these 5 parameters before and after every mudball deployment.

DO
💧 Dissolved Oxygen

Oxygen available for fish and aquatic life. Higher is healthier. Target: >7 mg/L

BOD
🦠 Biochemical Oxygen Demand

Amount of oxygen needed to break down organic matter. Lower is cleaner.

COD
⚗️ Chemical Oxygen Demand

Measures chemical pollutants. Lower indicates better water quality.

NH₃
🧪 Ammonia Nitrogen

Toxic to aquatic life at high levels. Indicates sewage or waste runoff.

WQI
📊 Water Quality Index

Combined score: Class I (>92.7) to Class V (<30.9). We target Class II+.
