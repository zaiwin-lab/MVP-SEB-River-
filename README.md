# Sungai Revive — River Rehabilitation Impact Platform

> **Maturity:** Interactive ESG concept demonstration · static multilingual programme experience

Sungai Revive is a four-language front-end concept for explaining, coordinating and communicating a community river-rehabilitation programme across education, field activities, environmental monitoring and ESG reporting.

**Live demonstration:** [sungai-revive.netlify.app](https://sungai-revive.netlify.app/)

## Business problem

River rehabilitation is a continuing programme rather than a one-day clean-up. It requires baseline evidence, agreed interventions, participant coordination, repeated measurements and credible reporting. These elements are often dispersed across presentations, spreadsheets, field notes and campaign materials. Sungai Revive explores how they could be organised into one accessible public experience.

## Intended users

- Programme managers and field coordinators
- Schools, teachers and students
- Community organisations and volunteers
- Environmental specialists and researchers
- CSR and ESG teams evaluating participation
- Management and reporting stakeholders

## Demonstrated capabilities

- English, Bahasa Melayu, Chinese and Iban interface switching
- River-awareness and education content
- Four-stage programme-roadmap presentation
- Toolkit, facilitator-guide and activity-resource concepts
- Mudball, riverbank and school-activity journeys
- Plain-language presentation of environmental technologies
- Water-quality parameter explanations
- Animated programme and ESG indicators
- Participation pathways for volunteers, schools, communities, companies and sponsors
- Responsive navigation and scroll-based visual effects

## Strategic value

The concept makes a complex environmental programme easier to discuss with schools, communities, technical advisers and potential funders. It can support requirements workshops and programme design before investment in a governed field-data, evidence and reporting platform.

## What is actually implemented

The repository contains a single static HTML application with embedded CSS and JavaScript. Language switching and animated counters run in the browser. There is no backend, form submission, database, identity system, sensor connection, evidence upload, geospatial register or reporting engine.

Figures displayed for targeted rivers, mudballs, schools, participants and ESG outcomes are presentation data—not verified operational achievements. Partner labels and footer statements referencing Sarawak Energy, Sarawak Government, schools, universities, NGOs or other bodies do not establish a confirmed partnership, sponsorship, commissioning or endorsement.

The interface uses “AI” language in its broader concept materials, but no AI model, API or automated environmental-analysis feature is implemented in the application.

## Responsible-use boundaries

- Water-quality, biochar, effective-microorganism, mudball and bioremediation claims require review by qualified environmental professionals and citations to appropriate evidence.
- Environmental interventions should not proceed from website content alone; they require baseline assessment, site approval, safe methods and monitoring plans.
- Impact reporting needs defined indicators, calibrated methods, sampling protocols, dates, locations, evidence custody and independent verification where appropriate.
- Programme statistics, partner names, target figures and testimonials must not be published as facts without authorised evidence.
- Production volunteer or school participation requires safeguarding, consent, privacy notices, risk assessments and emergency procedures.
- Environmental results should include uncertainty and adverse findings rather than only positive campaign narratives.
- Translations, especially technical environmental terms, require qualified human review.
- Third-party names, marks and programme materials require permission before operational use.

## Current limitations

- No real river, site or intervention register
- No verified baseline or time-series water-quality dataset
- No participant registration or consent workflow
- No evidence uploads, location mapping or chain of custody
- No authenticated management dashboard
- No downloadable operational reports
- No AI or sensor integration
- No cited scientific reference library inside the repository
- No automated tests, accessibility audit or production monitoring is evidenced
