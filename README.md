# River Rehabilitation Impact Platform

A client-neutral concept platform for planning, coordinating and reporting community-based river rehabilitation programmes.

## Purpose

Environmental rehabilitation projects involve more than a one-day activity. They require location planning, intervention records, evidence, community participation, repeated monitoring and credible impact reporting.

This prototype explores how those activities can be organised through a simple digital platform.

## Core Capabilities

- project and location overview;
- intervention-round planning;
- activity, participant and partner records;
- photo and field-evidence collection;
- water-condition and observation tracking;
- progress and outcome dashboards;
- awareness content and community updates;
- report and bulletin preparation support.

## Intended Users

Programme managers, field coordinators, community partners, environmental specialists, management and reporting teams.

## Programme Logic

1. Establish the location baseline.
2. Plan intervention rounds and responsibilities.
3. Record implementation and participation.
4. Monitor observations over time.
5. Consolidate evidence and outcomes.
6. Communicate results through reports and awareness materials.

## Status

**Independent concept prototype — not a production monitoring system.**

This repository demonstrates environmental programme-management capability. It does not imply commissioning, ownership, partnership or endorsement by any external organisation.

Any production version would require expert validation of environmental methods, approved monitoring indicators, data governance, stakeholder consent and formal project authority.
